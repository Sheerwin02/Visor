The science question

Curiosity drilled a ridge top, hollow bedrock and a transition area, and found clay in the ridge and carbonate in the hollow. NASA says it cannot yet explain why nodules sit along ridge walls and in hollows but not near the fractures.

One loop that touches all three contexts gives an astronaut the whole contrast in a single walk. NASA JPL article

Why this loop

    What this prototype is

    A planning sketch. The terrain is generated to match NASA's reported boxwork sizes (ridges 1 to 2 m tall, hollows 10 to 20 m across). The real build would use the USGS Gale 1 m elevation model (Calef and Parker, 2016) and Curiosity photos from the PDS.

    What it does not do yet

    • No real radiation data. A sol-by-sol dose scenario needs the MSL RAD archive.
    • Effort is a simple grade and surface model, not a measured suit or metabolic model.
    • The science score is a heuristic of mine, not a NASA metric.
